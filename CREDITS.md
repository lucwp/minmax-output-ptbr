# Credits and provenance

MinMax PT-BR Output is not a greenfield work. It was built from and materially adapts **Better Portuguese**, created by **Rafael Quintanilha**.

- Upstream skill: https://github.com/rafaelquintanilha/skills/tree/master/skills/better-portuguese
- Author: https://github.com/rafaelquintanilha

The relationship is structural, not merely inspirational. The MinMax runtime retains and evolves the upstream approach to semantic preservation, Portuguese-native composition, grammar review, punctuation review, and editorial deconstruction. Several reference files still make the Better Portuguese lineage explicit.

## What MinMax adds

The MinMax branch extends that foundation with a stricter hard-gate contract for semantic fidelity, selective progressive loading, explicit artifact calibration, a dedicated naturalness-and-register layer, and a regression suite designed to prevent over-editing and semantic drift during future maintenance.

## Linguistic references

The grammar and punctuation references also rely on research from:

**ALMEIDA, Napoleão Mendes de. _Gramática metódica da língua portuguesa_.**

The repository references both an 18th-edition research copy and the 2024 CDB publication for specific numbered paragraphs and punctuation material. Almeida is a bibliographic source for linguistic prescriptions, not an upstream skill author.

Accordingly, this repository does not assign an open-source license to upstream-derived material by assumption. Attribution records provenance but does not create licensing rights.
